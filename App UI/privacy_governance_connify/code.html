Our Privacy Standards

Version 2.4.0

I. Movement Privacy

Connify utilizes "Privacy Zones" around your home and work. Within these zones, tracking is strictly restricted to your local device unless a Critical SOS is triggered. We never build a heat-map of your daily routine.

II. Third-Party Policy

We have zero data-sharing agreements with advertisers or data brokers. Your information is never "for sale." We only interface with verified emergency responders during active safety events.

III. Encryption Protocols

All communications use TLS 1.3 encryption. Safety session data is salted and hashed with device-specific identifiers that prevent reverse-engineering of user paths by server administrators.

IV. Transparency Audits

Our core privacy logic is audited quarterly by independent security firms. We publish summarized results to our Trust Center to maintain absolute clarity on our data handling practices.
