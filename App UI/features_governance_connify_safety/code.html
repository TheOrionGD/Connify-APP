Privacy & Governance

Data Protocol

Minimal Logging

We don't sell your movements. Connify records only the telemetry required for live coordination. Historical data is purged every 24 hours unless explicitly saved for legal evidence.

Hardware Control

Device Governance

Manage which devices can initiate alerts. From Apple Watch to Android Auto, maintain a centralized registry of verified hardware permitted to access your safety network.

Edge Computing

On-Device Processing

Anomalous pattern detection (like sudden stops or off-route detours) is calculated locally on your device, not our servers, maintaining absolute privacy until an alert is triggered.
