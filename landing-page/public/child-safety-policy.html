Child Safety Standards & CSAM Policy

Connify Safety Network | Effective Date: September 18, 2026
Zero Tolerance Statement: Connify maintains a strict, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), child grooming, and any form of child exploitation or endangerment.

1. Compliance & Scope

Connify is a peer-to-peer safety response and community assistance mobile platform. We strictly enforce Google Play's Child Safety Standards, national laws, and international child protection regulations across all communication, profile data, and emergency distress dispatch channels.

2. In-App Reporting Mechanisms

Connify provides direct, accessible in-app reporting tools for users to report child safety concerns or inappropriate behavior:

  • 1-Tap Content & User Flagging: Users can tap the flag icon on any user profile, request details, or message thread to report child safety violations.
  • Immediate Incident Review: Flagged reports involving child safety are escalated immediately for urgent review and response.
  • In-App Support Portal: Users can submit safety concerns directly within the application via the Safety Hub settings panel.

3. Content Moderation & Enforcement

Upon receiving a report or detecting prohibited content related to CSAE/CSAM:

  • Immediate permanent suspension and ban of the offending user account and device fingerprint.
  • Removal and purging of associated content from active session relays.
  • Permanent blacklisting of account credentials and Ed25519 identity keys across the network.

4. Reporting to Regional & National Authorities

Connify fully complies with legal requirements to report known or suspected Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or exploitation to relevant authorities:

  • Reports are submitted to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline.
  • Immediate notification to regional law enforcement agencies and national cyber-crime response units when emergency or child safety threats are detected.
